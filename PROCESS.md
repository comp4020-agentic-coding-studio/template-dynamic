Markers follow your citations; they don't trawl the repo for evidence you didn't
point at. A citation is a link whose text is the commit hash or range and whose
target is this repo's commit or compare URL:

- one commit: [`a1b2c3d`](https://github.com/YOUR-ORG/YOUR-REPO/commit/a1b2c3d)
